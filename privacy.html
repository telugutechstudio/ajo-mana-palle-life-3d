Privacy Policy

Mana Palle Life 3D · Last updated: 19 October 2026 · తెలుగులో చదవండి

Summary

Mana Palle Life 3D is a free game. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. The game has no ads and no analytics.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (language, sound) are kept in your browser's local storage on your phone or computer. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using New game in the menu, deletes them.

Services we use

  • Netlify hosts the game (Netlify privacy policy). Like any web server, it may keep basic request logs (such as IP address) for a short time.
  • jsDelivr delivers the 3D graphics library (three.js) (jsDelivr privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts (Google privacy policy).

Children

The game is for a general audience. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/telugutechstudio/ajo-mana-palle-life-3d.
