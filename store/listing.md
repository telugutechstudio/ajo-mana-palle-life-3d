### Full description
```
Welcome to Mana Palle, a peaceful Telugu village in 3D! Walk around, help your neighbours and build a happy village life, one day at a time.

★ A DAY IN THE VILLAGE
• Draw water from the well, collect firewood and make chai on the chulha
• Collect eggs from the coop and milk the cow
• Pray at the temple and sell your produce at the kirana shop

★ FARMING
• Grow tomatoes, brinjal, chilli and corn: each season has its own crops
• Plant paddy saplings, flood the field from the canal and harvest golden rice
• Rain waters your crops for free!

★ ANIMALS
• Buy goats, a buffalo and ducks, feed them and get fresh milk and eggs

★ FUN AND GAMES
• Fishing at the village pond
• Play cricket with Chintu: hit fours and sixes!
• Ride the bullock cart to the town market
• Ride the giant wheel at the Sunday santa

★ FRIENDLY VILLAGERS
• Ramayya tatha, Lakshmi akka, Chintu, the sarpanch, the teacher, the postman and Chai Anna
• Help them with quests and make Mana Palle the Best Village of the district
• Listen to folk stories by the bonfire at night

★ SEASONS AND FESTIVALS
• Winter mist, summer heat and monsoon rain with thunder
• Celebrate Sankranti (kites!), Deepavali (crackers!), Ugadi and Vinayaka Chavithi

★ YOUR HOME
• Upgrade your hut to a tiled house and then a pakka illu
• Buy a fan, a radio and a TV

★ MADE FOR YOU
• Full Telugu and English
• 20 achievements to unlock
• Low graphics mode to save battery
• Backup code to move your game to another phone
• Small download, works offline, no ads, no account

Free to play. Your progress stays on your phone.
```

## Data safety and content rating

- **Data collected:** none. The game has no accounts, ads or analytics; progress is saved only on the phone.
- **Ads:** No.
- **Content rating questionnaire:** no violence, no gambling, no user chat, no personal data. Expect a rating of Everyone / 3+.

## Making the Android app (same steps as Business Empire)

1. Open https://www.pwabuilder.com, enter `https://ajo-mana-palle-life-3d.netlify.app` and choose **Package for stores → Android**.
2. Use package id `app.netlify.ajo_mana_palle_life_3d` (or any id you like; it can't change later) and keep the signing key file and passwords private.
3. Upload the `.aab` file to Play Console (Internal testing first).
4. Send me only the contents of `assetlinks.json` from the PWABuilder download. I'll add it to the site as `.well-known/assetlinks.json` so the app opens full screen without the browser bar.
